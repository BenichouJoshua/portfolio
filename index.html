Agentic AI · Operations case 01

A safe AI agent for operational risk

The problem

A manager tracking delivery risk has to open five systems — inventory, purchase orders, suppliers, alert history and the CRM — just to answer two questions: which deliveries are at risk, and does this one actually matter?

What I built

A FastAPI service where an LLM orchestrates a registry of deterministic business tools. It analyses operational data, cross-references the CRM to weigh technical risk against account status — strategic, at-risk or standard — and answers in the manager's own language, with its sources attached.

The hard part

Making the AI trustworthy. Every business rule — risk scoring, escalation — is deterministic Python, unit-tested, and never delegated to the model. The LLM may only call allowlisted tools; it never sees the database and never writes SQL. If the model is disabled or fails — bad key, quota, network — the agent degrades to deterministic routing instead of crashing, and reports whether AI was actually used.

The result

Answers “why is D-205 high risk?” with its evidence, surfaces every strategic account that needs escalation in one pass, and refuses out-of-scope requests by design.

Document automation · End-to-end case 02

End-to-end automation of a government immigration form

The problem

An immigration firm filled Canada's IMM5707 family-information form by hand — five to ten minutes per file, repetitive and error-prone — for every client.

What I built

An end-to-end pipeline that turns a client's submission into a finished, emailed PDF with no manual step. A form fires a webhook; Python normalizes the data and writes it into the database — idempotent, deduplicated; a FastAPI + Java/PDFBox worker generates the official form; the result is emailed to the chosen recipient.

The hard part

The IMM5707 is a dynamic XFA PDF that fights back. Sections must appear or hide by marital status, but Adobe silently discards any injected state whose checksum doesn't match the data. I reverse-engineered that undocumented validation through controlled A/B experiments, then drove visibility by byte-exact edits to the form template and correctly stripped the usage-rights signature so Adobe wouldn't freeze the form. The behaviour is locked by a two-layer regression suite across seven marital-status cases.

The result

Triggers generation of a correct, still-editable IMM5707 for any family composition, emailed automatically — the manual fill is gone.
